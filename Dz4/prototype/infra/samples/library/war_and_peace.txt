War and Peace — Chapter One (EN)

"Well, Prince, so Genoa and Lucca are now just private estates of the
Buonaparte family. And what do you think of this war with Austria?"

Anna Pavlovna Scherer, a lady of forty, greeted her guest. Napoleon, said
the prince, was advancing on Vienna. The conversation turned to politics,
court intrigues, and the latest gossip of Petersburg society.
